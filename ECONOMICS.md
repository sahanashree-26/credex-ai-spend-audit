# ECONOMICS

# Product Economics

## Core Value Proposition

The product helps teams identify unnecessary AI subscription spending.

If the platform saves even:

- $100/month
- $500/month
- or more

the ROI becomes immediately clear for startups and teams.

---

# Potential Business Model

## Free Tier

- basic AI spend audit
- limited report generation

Purpose:

- user acquisition
- sharing
- viral growth

---

## Pro Tier

Potential future features:

- billing integrations
- usage analytics
- team dashboards
- optimization tracking
- historical reports

Possible pricing:

- $15–49/month

---

## Enterprise Tier

Potential future enterprise features:

- admin controls
- SSO
- centralized AI governance
- multi-team analytics
- procurement optimization

Custom pricing model.

---

# Cost Structure

Current prototype costs are minimal because:

- frontend only
- no backend infrastructure
- deployed on Vercel free tier

Future costs would include:

- database hosting
- analytics infrastructure
- API integrations
- AI processing
- authentication systems

---

# Long-Term Opportunity

As companies adopt more AI subscriptions, AI SaaS cost management may become similar to traditional SaaS expense management platforms.
