# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target Users

Primary target users:

- startup founders
- small engineering teams
- indie hackers
- AI-heavy product teams

These users often subscribe to multiple AI tools quickly without visibility into overlapping costs.

## Problem

Many teams adopt:

- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Cursor
- Copilot
- Gemini

without tracking:

- overlapping functionality
- unused seats
- inefficient plan allocation

This creates silent SaaS cost expansion.
